# Data attribution

Source: data.org Financial Health Prediction Challenge, Zindi, with FinMark Trust.
https://zindi.world/competitions/dataorg-financial-health-prediction-challenge

The challenge states CC BY-SA 4.0 for its data:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Train.csv and Test.csv are unmodified supplied challenge files, renamed from the uploaded copies. VariableDefinitions.csv is also supplied unchanged (semicolon-delimited). No claim of ownership over challenge data is made. Derived data remain subject to the applicable share-alike terms.

Training: 9,618 labeled businesses; competition test: 2,405 unlabeled businesses. Four countries: Eswatini, Lesotho, Zimbabwe and Malawi. The challenge test labels are unavailable. Do not treat local validation as a leaderboard result.
